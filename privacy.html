MEOW HQ privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

MEOW HQ is a personal tool used only by its owner, Emil Abrera. It accesses the owner's own Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets) to help the owner manage it.

  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer and is not sold or shared with third parties.
  • Google user data is used only to provide the features the owner requests, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for MEOW HQ.
